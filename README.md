### Main project: Easy IGCSE

<a href="https://easyigcse.com">
  <img alt="Easy IGCSE: free IGCSE revision on the web, and an AI study system that works in any AI" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kirill-Chistov/igcse-revision-kit/main/docs/img/banner.svg" width="100%">
</a>

I'm sitting my IGCSEs in 2027, so I'm building the revision tool I wanted and couldn't find.

- **[easyigcse.com](https://easyigcse.com)** — a free revision website: notes cut to the exact specification, diagrams you can spin in 3D, quizzes, exam-style questions with full answers, a 3D practice lab, and a tracker for every specification point. No account, no ads.
- **[igcse-revision-kit](https://github.com/Kirill-Chistov/igcse-revision-kit)** — the same notes as an AI study system: a folder that turns Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini or any other AI into a tutor that knows your spec and exam dates, plans your week and remembers every session.
- **6 subjects · 164 lessons · 1,709 quiz questions · 1,537 specification points · 109 required practicals in 3D**
- Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Business (Edexcel International GCSE), Computer Science and Geography (Cambridge IGCSE). More subjects are added as they are written.
